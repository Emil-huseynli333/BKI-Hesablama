Privacy Policy

At bkihesablama.site, accessible from https://www.bkihesablama.site, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by bkihesablama.site and how we use it.

1. Log Files

bkihesablama.site follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files include internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks.

2. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

3. Data Protection Rights

We would like to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the following: The right to access, the right to rectification, and the right to erasure.

4. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. bkihesablama.site does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.
